Important

This checker is general information, not medical advice. It covers 20 common prescription groups and 55 everyday products at usual doses, and can't take account of your kidneys, liver, other conditions or exact doses. Never stop a prescribed medicine because of a colour here. Ask your doctor or pharmacist.
